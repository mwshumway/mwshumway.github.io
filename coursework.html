Boston University

PhD, Faculty of Computing and Data Sciences

Fall 2026 (in progress)
Complexity Theory (CAS CS 535, Mark Bun)

The capabilities and fundamental limits of efficient computation: which problems are inherently hard, and how resources such as time, space, randomness and interaction affect what can be computed.

Spring 2026
Introduction to Sequential Decision Making (CDS DS 592)

Design and analysis of algorithms for sequential decision making, focused on bandits and statistical learning theory: UCB, EXP3, linear and contextual bandits, and function approximation.

Multimodal Machine Learning (CAS CS 598, Bryan Plummer)

Representation, fusion, alignment and generation across modalities such as vision, language and signals, including multimodal transformers, interaction and quantification, with a semester-long team research project.

Fall 2025
Algorithmic Mechanism Design (CDS DS 574, Kira Goldner)

Computational perspectives applied to economic problems, and economic techniques applied to computer science: welfare-maximizing auctions, equilibria, and mechanism design for social good.

Algorithms for Machine Learning (CAS CS 599 E1, Alina Ene)

Seminar on efficient algorithms for building modern machine learning models at scale: adaptive gradient methods, dimensionality reduction, retrieval, attention and state space models, and model compression.
